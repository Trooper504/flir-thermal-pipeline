Hardware note: the FLIR E8-XT is a USB mass-storage instrument — it does not expose a UVC / DirectShow / V4L2 video interface, so OpenCV cannot enumerate it. On the Pi, MJPEG mode needs a real video device (check ls /dev/video*; a USB video grabber wired to the camera's video output appears there), otherwise the collector answers HTTP 503. Radiometric polling needs no video hardware: it streams genuine Planck-calibrated frames from /api/v1/thermal-frame, updated as fast as the Pi can extract a new capture (typically 0.5–2 s per shot, not the E8-XT's 9 Hz).
